# Qualification gate for SCF-01/OSS-01

No date-based or preference-based cutover is allowed. The open-source strategic runtime becomes primary only when it proves:

1. clean reconstruction of the complete event ledger, active-state projection and source graph from immutable Obzio-owned storage;
2. provider-neutral execution of commissions, tools, skills and result transactions;
3. interchangeable model adapters with no strategic logic hidden in a provider prompt or memory;
4. exact secret and authority separation;
5. replay-safe leases, fences, checkpoints, idempotency and result custody;
6. equal or superior independently accepted strategic performance on current and held-out Obzio workloads;
7. current-plan execution and continuous discovery at effective scale;
8. founder-interface continuity and decision interlock;
9. recovery from loss of any one provider runtime;
10. two successful successor generations and independent acceptance.

Cursor, SW and ChatGPT remain replaceable bindings throughout qualification. The role, ledger, schemas, evidence and strategy authority remain Obzio-owned.

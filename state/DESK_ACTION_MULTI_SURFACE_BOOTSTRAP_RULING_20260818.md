Obzio's commercial direction is not the governance corpus itself. Obzio builds monetisable agent-first capability. The earlier blanket formulation that tools should receive only outcomes and never method is rejected. The correct rule is sequencing:

- before a method is established, give the native surface the objective, evidence requirements and genuine boundaries, and let it propose or exercise its strongest method;
- after a method has been researched, tested and accepted for a bounded workload, encode and specify that method to tools so it can be repeated and evaluated.

Wrong or premature method is the defect; method specification itself is not.

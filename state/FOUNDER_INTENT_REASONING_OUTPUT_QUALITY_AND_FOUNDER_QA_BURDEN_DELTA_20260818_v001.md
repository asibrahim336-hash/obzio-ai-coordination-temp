## 1. Controlling effect

This delta captures Ahmed's correction after the high-competence commissioning delta.

The immediate trigger was a low-value explanatory caveat that stated an obvious proposition and consumed founder attention without changing a decision. Ahmed identified the deeper problem: he should not be required to act as the routine detector of banal caveats, weak reasoning, unnecessary qualification, low-value padding, semantic drift or other output-quality defects.

This delta does not restart completed work or prescribe one evaluator topology. It strengthens the quality standard and the existing independent reasoning-quality requirement.

## 2. FOUNDER-EXPRESSED

### 2.1 Banal caveats and obvious defensive padding are quality defects

Consequential Obzio outputs should not spend attention restating propositions that are already obvious, uncontested and decision-irrelevant merely to appear cautious or complete.

Examples include caveats that do not alter the action, uncertainty, authority, evidence boundary or decision.

The required standard is not brevity for its own sake. It is **information density and decision value**.

Where a caveat is genuinely material — because it changes a boundary, action, uncertainty, legal/technical consequence or interpretation — state it. Where it adds no decision value, remove it.

### 2.2 Ahmed must not be the routine output-quality detector

Ahmed should not have to repeatedly identify:

- banal or obvious caveats;
- fake sophistication or defensive padding;
- repetition without new decision value;
- weak reasoning presented with high confidence;
- unnecessary restriction or authority shrinkage;
- semantic drift from founder intent;
- generic answers that fail to exploit a stronger downstream model;
- missing assumptions or alternatives;
- unsupported execution claims; or
- outputs that sound polished but do not improve the operating system.

The operating system must detect and reduce these defects before they consume founder attention.

### 2.3 Quality control must be substantive, not another bureaucracy

A quality function that produces long low-value commentary about minor stylistic defects recreates the same failure.

Quality challenge should focus on defects that materially affect:

- decision quality;
- founder intent fidelity;
- authority;
- execution quality;
- evidence quality;
- route quality;
- operating friction;
- strategic clarity; or
- founder cognitive burden.

The objective is to remove low-value content and bad reasoning, not to create another reporting layer.

### 2.4 Producer quality is a standing obligation

Every producer model/function is responsible for first-pass quality control before return or dispatch.

It should remove:

- redundant caveats;
- restatement of settled premises;
- decorative governance language;
- unnecessary disclaimers;
- repeated context that is already durably recoverable;
- empty praise or agreement;
- generic conclusions that do not change a decision; and
- process narration that does not help the recipient act or evaluate.

Independent challenge supplements producer discipline; it does not excuse poor producer output.

### 2.5 Founder-facing bandwidth is scarce operating capacity

Founder attention is a constrained resource. Consequential returns should maximise useful strategic, evidential and operating content per unit of attention.

This means surfacing:

- real discoveries;
- conflicts;
- consequential uncertainty;
- strong alternatives;
- genuine founder choices;
- useful discussion topics;
- operating changes;
- actual owner acts; and
- evidence that changes what Obzio should do.

Do not consume founder bandwidth with content that the system can safely infer, recover or discard itself.

### 2.6 Quality defects must become operational learning

When Ahmed or another evaluator identifies a recurring quality defect, the system should not merely correct the sentence.

The defect must be classified, propagated and used to improve the relevant:

- execution profile;
- prompt;
- source package;
- output contract;
- evaluator rubric;
- route;
- model allocation;
- skill; or
- operating rule.

Repeated founder correction of the same defect is evidence that the learning did not propagate.

## 3. FOUNDER-CONFIRMED

1. Low-value obvious caveats and defensive padding should be treated as quality defects and purged from consequential outputs.
2. Ahmed should not be the routine quality-control layer for model reasoning and output.
3. Quality control must itself be high-signal and must not become another verbose bureaucratic layer.
4. Founder-facing communication should optimise for decision value and useful information density.
5. Recurring quality defects must change the operating system rather than being corrected only locally.

## 4. EMPIRICAL-CLAIM

The following are founder-observed and require broader measurement before being generalised quantitatively:

- Obzio outputs have repeatedly included banal caveats, low-value qualification and defensive padding;
- Ahmed has repeatedly detected reasoning, framing and authority defects that upstream models did not catch themselves;
- this manual QA burden is tiring and reduces confidence in the operating level;
- some quality failures recur after prior correction, indicating incomplete learning propagation.

These are current failure observations, not a claim that every output or model has the same defect rate.

## 5. TECHNICAL-HYPOTHESIS

The following remain candidate mechanisms rather than founder-selected architecture:

- a distinct independent reasoning-and-output-quality evaluator;
- producer-side automatic output compression against a decision-value rubric;
- model-specific quality profiles and defect registries;
- automated detection of redundant caveats, duplicated reasoning and authority shrinkage;
- pre-dispatch challenge for consequential outputs with lighter sampling for routine work;
- adaptive evaluator depth by consequence, model history and defect rate.

## 6. MODEL-RECOMMENDATION

1. Extend the existing `INDEPENDENT-REASONING-QUALITY-01` function to cover **reasoning quality + output quality + founder-bandwidth protection**, rather than creating a separate bureaucracy by default.
2. Use a compact defect taxonomy including at least: `BANAL_CAVEAT`, `DEFENSIVE_PADDING`, `REPETITION`, `FAKE_SOPHISTICATION`, `WEAK_REASONING`, `UNSUPPORTED_CLAIM`, `AUTHORITY_SHRINKAGE`, `SEMANTIC_DRIFT`, `GENERIC_COMMISSIONING`, `PLAN_AS_EXECUTION`, `LOW_DECISION_VALUE`.
3. Require consequential founder-facing returns to pass a final question: `What material decision, understanding, action or uncertainty does each retained section change?` Remove sections with no answer unless required for provenance or safety.
4. Feed repeated defect patterns into model/surface capability registers and execution profiles so routing and autonomy reflect observed quality.
5. Prefer targeted independent review for material outputs and statistical/sampled review for routine outputs rather than reviewing everything at maximum cost.

These are recommendations for Strategic Control/evaluation design, not founder-bound topology.

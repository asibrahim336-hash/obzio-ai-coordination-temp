## 4. SW disclosure control

A future SW disclosure trial must ask explicitly for every product-visible item:

1. skills used;
2. tools called;
3. displayed reasoning/rationale transcript available to the operator;
4. source/context package actually loaded;
5. what was summarised;

7. unresolved assumptions;
8. errors, retries and route changes;

The return is then compared against native tool/state receipts and outcome evidence. Observation is secondary but mandatory where it can expose inconsistency or negative space. A separate challenger inspects what may never have entered the reasoning or source package.

## 5. Assumption-tracking control

Every consequential deliverable ends with:

```text
ASSUMPTIONS
- assumption_id:
  proposition:
  basis:
  state: OPEN / VERIFIED / KILLED / SUPERSEDED / BOUNDED-WORKING-PREMISE
  owner:
  verification_or_kill_route:
  downstream_dependency:
  next_recheck:
```

Rules:

- basis is stated where the assertion appears, not deferred only to the final block;
- the assumptions block includes every material unresolved premise introduced or inherited by the deliverable;
- each open assumption is written to the shared assumption register;
- successor deltas preserve it until closed;
- a source receipt, acknowledgement or model agreement does not verify it;
- a killed premise records which downstream objects were corrected; and
- no assumption is converted into founder intent or active strategy by repetition.

### Design requirement

Reduce Ahmed's role as the detector of bad model reasoning.

### Current working mandate

Before consequential founder-facing return or destination dispatch, a function distinct from the producer tests:

- claim basis and claim-fit;
- assumptions and unverified inferences;
- omitted alternatives, stakeholders and failure modes;
- semantic fidelity to founder intent;
- authority/access separation;
- destination fit and dereference assumptions;
- currentness and supersession;
- whether evidence supports the exact action proposed; and
- whether a required operating change actually landed durably.

Self-check is a producer responsibility, not independent acceptance. Cross-model review is useful only when its sources, assumptions, methods or incentives provide meaningful independence.

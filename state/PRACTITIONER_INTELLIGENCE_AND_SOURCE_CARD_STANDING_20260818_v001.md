## 1. Purpose

Practitioner and community intelligence is a continuous evidence function. It discovers serious operators, end users, maintainers, issue reporters, repositories, demonstrations, failures and postmortems; converts them into proposition-specific source cards; and propagates useful findings into project source packs, commissions, tests and route decisions.

This function does not elevate popularity, follower count or community repetition into evidence. Reliability is assessed by proposition, demonstrated experience, reproducibility, incentives, recency, directness and applicability to Obzio.

## 2. Minimum source-card schema

Each card records:

`card_id | identity/candidate identity | domain | source class | exact locator | demonstrated experience | proposition/claim | method/evidence | incentives/conflicts | reliability by proposition | contradictions | validity/recheck | Obzio relevance | operating object changed | rights/sensitivity | lifecycle`.

Where identity is unresolved, the card remains an unresolved candidate and no person is guessed.

## 5. Consumption rule

A practitioner source is consumed only when it:

- changes a current prompt, route, project source pack, skill, test, acceptance criterion or operating rule; or
- produces a justified no-change/rejection record.

A card is not an endorsement. A card records what the source can and cannot support.

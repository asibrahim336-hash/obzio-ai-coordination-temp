## Founder outcome

Return an evidence-backed target-state recommendation, procurement decision packet and approximately seven-day executable transition plan.

- that SW/Metamate is the primary research or co-worker surface;
- that Cursor is merely temporary scaffolding or the future control plane;
- that GitHub is the permanent coordination architecture;
- that Operator D remains the strategic integrator;
- that Fable 5, Opus 5, GPT-5.6 Sol or any named model is the winner; or
- that a provider-native agent object can be transferred unchanged into Obzio-owned infrastructure.

Those are candidates and hypotheses to evaluate.

## Method authority and operating-fitness rule

Use the strongest current method, surfaces, models, research lanes, tools, temporary agents and experiments justified by decision value. No arbitrary lane, worker, task or step cap applies. Do not manufacture volume.

Before substantive work, every lead lane must state its operating fitness:

`surface/account | model/mode | tools | source access | destination-write ability | context completeness | connection/interface reliability | working-room risk | stronger alternative if any | safe work that can continue`

If a material route defect exists, expose it immediately. Recommend and use the stronger legitimate route where authorised. Do not silently reduce scope, compress the research into one model, continue through poor context, or make Ahmed discover the defect after the work degrades.

## Workstream A — physical and computational package

Compare at least the credible classes, eliminating any only with evidence:

- capable workstation-only;
- workstation plus local inference/server node;
- hybrid local compute plus elastic cloud/provider burst;
- provider-heavy transitional setup with owned state/control; and
- any stronger architecture discovered.

Assess:

- CPU, GPU/accelerator, memory, storage and networking;
- laptop/workstation/server split;
- power, thermals, noise, reliability and physical footprint;
- remote access from Ahmed's actual devices;
- availability, warranty, support and upgrade route;
- inference, training/adaptation and concurrent-agent capacity;
- backup and recovery;
- one-time and recurring cost; and
- expected useful life and exit treatment.

State assumptions and measured or source-supported workload units. Do not infer a hardware purchase from ambition alone.

A 2D/3D surface is a founder-set baseline direction, but implementation and timing are selected by demonstrated task utility, accessibility, latency and commercial value. Do not build spectacle as proof.

## Failure conditions

The programme fails if it:

- promotes a surface allocation or operator seat into founder intent;
- continues through a materially poor connection, context, model, tool or working-room state without reporting it;
- arbitrarily limits research lanes, workers, tasks or steps;
- asks Ahmed to retrieve or relay recoverable files or merge outputs;
- treats a filename, search hit or summary as source consumption;
- seeks hidden chain of thought;
- chooses a model, provider, hardware package or topology from preference or marketing;
- ignores serious practitioner/operator evidence;
- claims portability without substitution/rebuild evidence;
- claims continuity without write/read-back and successor testing;
- churns a depended-upon or unclassified repository;
- hides material dissent;
- self-certifies; or
- causes unauthorised outreach, spend, credential disclosure, production mutation or restricted-data movement.

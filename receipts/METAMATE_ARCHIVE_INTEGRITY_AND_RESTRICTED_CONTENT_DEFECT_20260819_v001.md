## 5. EMPIRICAL-CLAIM — restricted distribution content

The inspected historical records contain private Metamate-space and Drive/account locators. Under the current boundary against identity-data disclosure, these raw records must not be copied into a general operator launch attachment or broad project-source distribution.

This is a distribution restriction, not an instruction to destroy or ignore the originals. Preserve the protected originals. When their substantive content is needed in a wider internal lane, create a separately named, byte-counted and hashed redacted derivative, identify the original by its existing file identity, and state what was removed and why.

No secret value, credential, token, private space ID, Drive link or account locator is reproduced in this receipt.

Overview

Free AI sidebar with ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini & DeepSeek. 100+ commands to automate, monitor prices, summarize & write.
🧬 Bring all AI models into one sidebar. Make AI aware of the web page you're on. Automate search, summarize videos & PDFs, monitor prices, and extract data. Trusted by over 400.000 users and companies.

🌟 CALL HARPA WITH [Alt+A]

• 🎬 Summarize hours long YouTube videos into easy-to-read actionable summaries
• 🧠 Assist your research, work, study or language learning with deep web search features
• 📎 Extract data from images, summarize files and PDFs
• 💌 Write and polish Emails in your tone of voice
• 👍 Help with Social Media Marketing on X/Twitter, Instagram, Reddit, Medium and Facebook, writing post captions, hashtags, direct message replies
• ✍️ Spellcheck and translate text information
• 🚀 Run over 100+ predefined page-aware commands to save time
• 💰 Saves you money by monitoring prices so you could buy when they are low

🌟 HOW HARPA IS DIFFERENT FROM CHAT BOTS

• ✅️️️️️️️ Always at hand, contextual and customizeable. Press [Alt+A] or double-slash // to bring it up on any tab.
• ✅️️️️️️️ Practical. Combines all major AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and others) in one product and subscription.
• ✅️️️️️️️ Fast. Our cloud AI connections are faster than the web chatbots, not geo-locked, not rate limited, and experience less downtime.
• ✅️️️️️️️ Much less hallucinations. Our built-in commands are designed to be as hallucination free as possible, providing accurate answers along with citations, grounded in the source materials.
• ✅️️️️️️️ Privacy-oriented. HARPA keeps your data locally, does not store logs, and relies on AI APIs that do not use your data for training.
• ✅️️️️️️️ Dev and Team-friendly. Bring your own keys or connect it to OpenRouter and Portkey APIs.

🌟 AI AUTOMATION

HARPA features a powerful hybrid AI engine that understands the structure and semantics of the web pages. It can:

• ✅ Automate websites, e.g. for data scraping and automating routine tasks via AI commands.
• ✅ Monitor Web Data and Prices to detect and get notified on web page updates — helpful to detect price drops, product availability updates and changes in competitor websites.
• ✅ Trigger Make.com scenarios, Zapier and n8n webhooks.
• ✅ HARPA Grid API to run custom HARPA commands and automate your browser.
• ✅ Extract data from web pages in CSV and JSON formats.

HARPA AI is a new breed of AI automation products and is designed to give you web browsing and automation superpowers.

🌟 HOW TO INSTALL & USE

1️⃣ Click "Add to Chrome" to install the plugin.
2️⃣ Navigate to a web page you want to summarize, monitor or extract data from, e.g. a product catalog.
3️⃣ Click the plugin icon in the Chrome 🧩 toolbar, a sidebar will appear. Ask HARPA anything, drag & drop PDF, or switch to AUTOMATE tab to automate or monitor the page.

🔐 PRIVACY BY DESIGN

HARPA is privacy-centric. It reads web pages only upon your explicit actions, including: asking it a question in a page-aware mode, using page parameters or page-aware commands and running page monitors.

Our privacy policy is publicly available at: https://harpa.ai/privacy

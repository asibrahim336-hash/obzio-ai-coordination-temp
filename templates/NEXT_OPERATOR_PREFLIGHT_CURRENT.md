# Current successor preflight

An authorised successor must answer every item from repository state without asking Ahmed to reconstruct it.

1. What institutional function do I occupy?
2. What is my appointment ID and status?
3. What commission is active, and what earlier launch evidence does it continue?
4. What authority envelope applies? Which acts are expressly bounded?
5. What runtime binding is current, and why does it have no authority effect?
6. Which aliases are historical, ambiguous or prohibited for routing?
7. Which one instruction stack is current?
8. Which predecessor controls were migrated, and which files remain evidence only?
9. What is the output, evaluation and independent-acceptance route?
10. What open work, failures and access defects continue without becoming global gates?

## Pass conditions

- Exact IDs and paths are returned for function, appointment, commission, authority, runtime and stack.
- The successor distinguishes `EXECUTED_AND_CONTINUING` from the separate destination byte-identity defect.
- The successor does not call itself Operator D, Claude extension, principal AI operator or a provider/model name.
- The successor preserves standing internal authority without inventing founder approval loops.
- No bounded external/owner/protected act is inferred from technical access.
- `python scripts/check_operator_taxonomy.py` passes.
